# 🏥 Health Technology Assessment (HTA) Framework
**Nova Scotia Department of Health and Wellness (DHW)**

> **Executive Synopsis:** A standardized, data-driven governance framework establishing objective capital allocation criteria for health technology investments ($> \$250,000$). Designed to align provincial spending with the *Action Plan for Health*, this methodology balances clinical efficacy with lifecycle economics, health equity, and environmental sustainability.

---

## 🎯 Strategic Purpose

Health care capital allocation frequently suffers from subjective prioritization and unquantified lifecycle costs. This framework eliminates evaluation bias by introducing a **4-Stage Governance Workflow** and a **Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA)** index to evaluate every major medical device, digital platform, and infrastructure expansion prior to Treasury Board submittal.

---

## ⚡ Core Framework Architecture

### 1. The 4-Stage Governance Lifecycle
$$\text{Strategic Intake} \longrightarrow \text{Technical Assessment (Clinical \& Financial)} \longrightarrow \text{MCDA Scoring} \longrightarrow \text{Governance Sign-Off}$$

* **Intake & Technical Vetting:** Projects pass baseline regulatory and cybersecurity screening before undergoing independent clinical safety and total cost of ownership (TCO) financial modeling.
* **MCDA Indexing:** Proposals are quantitatively scored across 10 sub-metrics to generate a normalized **Strategic Priority Score (0–100)**.

### 2. Weighted Evaluation Rubric

$$\text{Final Score } (S) = \sum_{i=1}^{10} \left( \text{Score}_i \times \text{Weight}_i \right)$$

* 🩺 **Clinical Quality & Capacity (30%):** Clinical efficacy/safety (10%), frontline workflow/burnout mitigation (10%), and wait-list reduction (10%).
* 💰 **Fiscal Accountability (30%):** Cost-utility ($\text{ICER} < \$50\text{k/QALY}$) (10%), full 7-year TCO transparency (10%), and cash-releasing downstream offsets (10%).
* ⚖️ **Health Equity & Access (25%):** Rural/zone geographic parity (10%), cultural/structural inclusivity (10%), and system interoperability (5%).
* 🌿 **System Sustainability (15%):** Lifecycle carbon footprint/plastics reduction (7.5%) and supply chain resilience (7.5%).

---

## 🚦 Capital Decision Triage

| Tier | Priority Score | Action & Governance Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1** | **Score $\ge$ 85** | 🟢 **Accelerated Authorization:** Fast-tracked for immediate capital allocation and provincial deployment. |
| **Tier 2** | **Score 65–84** | 🟡 **Conditional Optimization:** Strong strategic merit with localized deficits; requires project remediation prior to funding. |
| **Tier 3** | **Score < 65** | 🔴 **Deferral / Rejection:** Fails strategic alignment or fiscal thresholds; capital denied. |
